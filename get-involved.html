How a Partnership Begins

A simple, three-step process for exploring a partnership with Vision Bags Uganda.

1

Tell Us About Yourself

Share a bit about your organization, your interests and the type of partnership you'd like to explore.

2

Discuss Together

We'll be in touch to discuss a suitable opportunity, ask questions and share what we're able to do.

3

Agree on a Plan

Once we find the right fit, we agree on a clear plan, timing and next steps together.

Start a Conversation
