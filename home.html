Rooted in Seeta, Mukono since 2014

Vision Bags Uganda is a community-based organization that started in 2014 in Seeta, Mukono, with one project: training and making quality African-print bags of all types — that's why the name Vision “Bags”.

As we prospered and our impact grew, we engaged in more lifelong practical projects in line with wealth creation and life empowerment: tailoring (bags, jackets, masks, wallets), reusable pads, beading, candles, bar and liquid soap, and psycho-social support.

With social responsibility at our core, we focus our charitable effort on orphans, persons with disabilities, youth and school dropouts, widows, single mothers and the elderly.

How Our Approach Works

Our approach reflects how we intend to work: practical skills, production, and reinvesting returns into community activities. This is presented as our intended methodology, not a guaranteed outcome.

1

Skills Development

Participants learn practical, marketable skills through hands-on training in tailoring, bag making and related crafts.

2

Production

Trained participants produce bags, wallets and other handmade items, applying their skills in real production contexts.

3

Reinvestment

Returns from product sales are intended to support ongoing community activities, subject to organizational approval.

From training to self-sustenance

“I started training in bags making at Vision Bags in 2015. I mastered the skill and now I own my own bags company in Nantabulirwa, which has grown to give me self-sustenance in life. From that time to date, Vision Bags has been very supportive in providing marketing skills, procurement advice and more.”

Solomon Lubwama Director, True Bags · Seeta, Mukono
