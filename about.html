Our Story

Rooted in Seeta, Mukono

Vision Bags Uganda began in Seeta, Mukono. According to the organization, it was established in 2014. These details remain subject to organizational verification and will be confirmed before publication.

The organization works alongside people in the community, focusing on practical skills, community participation and sustainable income pathways that support local livelihoods.

More detailed history — milestones, early activities and the people who shaped the organization — will appear here once approved and verified by Vision Bags Uganda.

Our Approach

How practical skills, production and sustainable income can support community activities. This is presented as our intended methodology, not a guaranteed outcome.

1

Practical Skills

Participants learn marketable skills through hands-on training in tailoring, bag making and related crafts.

2

Production

Trained participants apply their skills in real production contexts, crafting bags, wallets and other handmade items.

3

Sustainable Income

Income from product sales is intended to support ongoing community activities, subject to organizational approval.
